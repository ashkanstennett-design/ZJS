# Repository instructions

This repository supports ZJS's doctoral mainline in Petroleum and Natural Gas Engineering.

## Mandatory context for related work

Before planning, executing, reviewing, or packaging a task related to unconventional reservoirs, hydraulic fracturing, Petrel, fracturing materials, conductivity or damage, fracture-scale modeling, multiscale transfer, machine learning, material selection, or inverse design, read and apply:

- `.agents/skills/context-zjs-doctoral-mainline/SKILL.md`
- Its linked reference when the task requires research framing, cross-scale linkage, or evidence-maturity judgment.

Keep the chain visible where relevant: reservoir and engineering constraints -> material microstructure, damage, and conductivity -> fracture-scale transfer -> material-fracture-reservoir prediction and optimization -> reservoir-oriented material selection and inverse design.

Do not force unrelated tasks into the mainline. Do not overstate observations, models, predictions, or preliminary Petrel results.

## Completion rule

At the end of every substantive mainline-related task, evaluate whether the completed work contains a reusable, validated workflow, decision rule, schema, calculation or script, checklist, template, or failure-recovery pattern.

- If yes, create or update a focused skill under `.agents/skills/`, validate it, and register it in `SKILL_INDEX.md`.
- If no, do not create skill clutter; record the task result in the requested deliverable only.
- A summary skill must preserve scope, inputs, outputs, assumptions, evidence boundaries, verification, and stopping conditions.
- Never treat a task request as permission to push to a remote, publish externally, or include secrets or sensitive raw data.
